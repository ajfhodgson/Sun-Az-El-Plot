🌞 Solar Position Plotter

URL Parameters: The URL accepts query parameters for Latitude (lat), Longitude (lon), Declination (dec), Hours offset (off), Time interval (int), Satellite zoom (sat, 0 = none), Motto (motto), Inner colour (c1) and Outer colour (c2). Clicking Generate rebuilds this URL from the fields and reloads the page; the QR code bottom left encodes the same URL.
Example: https://ajfhodgson.github.io/Sun-Az-El-Plot/Polar-Sun-Az-El-Plot.html?lat=51.1788&lon=-1.8262&dec=180&off=0&int=15&sat=17&c1=ff0000&c2=0000ff&motto=Stonehenge

How to read this chart:

  • The sun's position is shown following the figure-of-eight 'analemma' for specific times of the day elevation angles
  • The figure of eight is due to solar time being variously behind and ahead of clock time ('mean time')
  • There is a dot for every day of the year, plotted radially (azimuth/bearing round the circle and elevation along the radius
  • The black circle represents the horizon (elevation = 0°) - the sun goes below during night-time
  • Months are indicated by colours, Jul - Dec and Jan - Jun are Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple in sequence
